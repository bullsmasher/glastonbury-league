How ties are broken

Teams are ranked by win percentage. Teams with the same record are grouped, and each team’s games against the others in that group are pooled into one head-to-head record. The higher head-to-head percentage ranks first; a team that played no one in the group counts as .000. If that’s still equal, points for decides it. The “vs Sleeper” column shows how each team moved compared with Sleeper’s points-for tiebreaker.

Data loads live from Sleeper every time this page opens, so it reflects Sleeper’s numbers as soon as the week is scored (typically Tuesday morning).
